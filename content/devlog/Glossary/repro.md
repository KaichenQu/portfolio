# repro

**Full name**: reproduce / reproduction

Reliably reproducing a bug. A stable repro is the prerequisite for debugging: without one, there's no way to verify whether a fix actually works. A minimal repro means trimming the trigger conditions down to the smallest set that still reproduces it.

Example usage:
> Give me repro steps so I can actually dig in — I can't reproduce it on my end right now.
