# p99

**Full name**: 99th percentile

Sort a set of numbers (usually latencies) and take the value at the 99% position: 99% of requests are faster than it, the slowest 1% are slower. Reflects "worst-case experience" far better than the average, since the average gets pulled down by the mass of fast requests and hides the long tail. Same idea behind p50 (median), p95, p999.

Example usage:
> Average of 20ms looks fine, but p99 is at 2s — there's a long-tail problem.
