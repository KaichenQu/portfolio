# SLA

**Full name**: Service Level Agreement

An externally committed service metric, e.g. "99.9% availability." 99.9% means no more than about 8.7 hours of downtime per year. The stricter internal target above an SLA is [[SLO]]; the actual measured value is [[SLI]].

Example usage:
> This endpoint has a 99.95% SLA — breach it and there's a penalty to pay.
