# MTTR

**Full name**: Mean Time To Recovery

The average time from an incident occurring to the service being restored — a measure of how fast you can stop the bleeding. The counterpart metric, MTBF, is mean time between failures. Lowering MTTR is usually more realistic than chasing zero errors.

Example usage:
> Make rollback one click first — MTTR can drop from half an hour to 2 minutes.
