# RCA

**Full name**: Root Cause Analysis

Doesn't stop at the surface symptom, but traces through to the actual cause of the problem. A common technique is asking "why" repeatedly (5 Whys) until you reach the root cause. The "root cause" section in a case note is RCA's output.

Example usage:
> Roll back first to stop the bleeding — do the RCA at tomorrow's postmortem.
